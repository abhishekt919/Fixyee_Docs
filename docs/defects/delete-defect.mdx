## Delete a Defect

1. Navigate to **Defects**.
2. Locate the defect you want to delete.
3. In the **Actions** column, click the **three-dot menu (⋮)**.
4. Select **Delete**.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

:::warning
Deleting a defect is permanent and cannot be undone.
:::
