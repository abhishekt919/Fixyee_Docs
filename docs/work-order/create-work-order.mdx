## In this article

Workspace owners can get an overview of all teamspaces in the workspace, modify their settings, and access additional management tools 🪂

---

## Teamspace settings

Workspace owners can manage settings for all teamspaces in the workspace.

To manage teamspace settings:

1. Go to your left sidebar and click **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Teamspaces** tab.

There are also settings that can be configured at the teamspace level. Some of these settings are available on Enterprise workspaces only.

:::info

**Note:** 
- In addition to workspace-wide settings, each teamspace has its own configurable settings. Refer to the **Intro to Teamspaces** article for more information.

:::

---

## Manage default teamspaces

Workspace owners can designate default teamspaces. All current and new workspace members will automatically join these teamspaces.


- Search for and add any teamspace you own to the list of default teamspaces.
- Click **Update** to save your changes.

:::info

**Note:**

- Turning a teamspace into a default teamspace automatically adds every workspace member to it.
- Converting a default teamspace back into a regular teamspace removes all workspace members from it.

:::

To remove a default teamspace:

1. Click the **×** next to the teamspace name.
2. Click **Update** to save your changes.

:::tip

Most workspaces only need one default teamspace. We recommend having no more than three default teamspaces, even in large organizations.

:::

## Manage teamspace creation

Choose who can create teamspaces within your workspace.

### Restrict teamspace creation

Enable **Limit teamspace creation to only workspace owners** to allow only workspace owners to create teamspaces.

You can view workspace owners from the **Members** tab.

### Allow all members to create teamspaces

Disable **Limit teamspace creation to only workspace owners** to allow any workspace member to create teamspaces.

---

## Manage existing teamspaces in your workspace

### View all existing teamspaces

Within the Teamspaces settings page, you can view all teamspaces you have access to.

The following information is displayed:

#### Owners

Owners of the teamspace.

#### Members

Members of the teamspace. Click the field to expand and view the full member list.

#### Access

Determines who can access the teamspace.

- **Open** — Anyone can join and view the content in the teamspace.
- **Closed** — Everyone can see that the teamspace exists, but they must be invited to join.
- **Private** — Only members and owners can access or invite others. The teamspace is hidden from non-members.

#### Updated

Displays when the teamspace was last updated.

Click the column header to sort teamspaces chronologically.

You can also:

- Search for teamspaces.
- Create new teamspaces.
- Filter by status (**Active** or **Archived**).
- Filter by owner.
- Filter by access level.

## Take ownership of a teamspace

Workspace owners on the Enterprise plan can view, join, and take ownership of any teamspace.

1. Go to your left sidebar and click **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Teamspaces** tab.
3. Click the **•••** menu on the right side of the teamspace row.
4. Choose to join as a member or owner.

---

## Archive a teamspace

Workspace owners can archive a teamspace.

1. Hover over the teamspace name in the sidebar.
2. Click the **•••** menu.
3. Select **Archive teamspace**.

:::warning

A teamspace can be archived but cannot be permanently deleted.

:::

---

## Restore archived teamspaces

Workspace owners who are also owners of a teamspace can restore archived teamspaces.

1. Go to your left sidebar and click **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Teamspaces** tab.
3. Select the **Archived** filter.
4. Click the **•••** menu next to the archived teamspace.
5. Select **Restore teamspace**.

The teamspace and all of its content will be restored for users who previously had access.

---

## Manage teamspaces without an owner

If a teamspace does not have an owner, workspace owners can assign a new one.

1. Go to your left sidebar and click **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Teamspaces** tab.
3. Use the **Owner** filter and select **Teamspaces with no owners**.
4. Click the **•••** menu beside the teamspace.
5. Assign a new owner.
